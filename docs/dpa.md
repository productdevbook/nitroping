# NitroPing Data Processing Addendum

Last updated: 2026-09-11

This document is a deployment-aligned DPA template for NitroPing Cloud. It should be accepted together with the applicable commercial agreement and completed with the legal entity and contact details of the service operator before customer use.

## 1. Roles

The customer is the controller (or processor acting for its own controller) of feedback data submitted through its projects. The NitroPing Cloud operator is the processor for that data and processes it only to provide, secure, operate, and improve the contracted service. The operator remains controller of account, billing, fraud-prevention, and service-analytics data where applicable.

## 2. Subject matter and duration

Processing covers feedback collection, attachment storage, moderation, comments, notifications, webhooks, roadmap/changelog publication, usage measurement, support, export, and deletion for the duration of the customer account and the applicable retention or deletion period.

## 3. Data and data subjects

Data may include identifiers and contact details, product feedback, technical context, identifiers supplied in approved metadata, and uploaded files. Data subjects may include the customer's users, employees, contractors, and visitors. Customers must not submit special-category data unless they have a lawful basis and an appropriate instruction and configuration.

## 4. Processor obligations

The operator will maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures, restrict personnel access, keep tenant scope in repository queries, protect credentials, sign webhooks, protect attachment access, assist with data-subject requests, notify the customer of a confirmed personal-data breach without undue delay, and delete or return customer data at termination subject to legal retention requirements.

## 5. Subprocessors

The current list is maintained in [`subprocessors.md`](./subprocessors.md). The operator will provide notice of material changes and give the customer a reasonable opportunity to object where required by applicable law.

## 6. International transfers

The customer and operator must complete the applicable transfer mechanism and supplementary measures for the customer's deployment and data locations. The default NitroPing configuration uses EU-jurisdiction Cloudflare storage resources where available; this does not by itself replace a transfer assessment.

## 7. Customer instructions

The customer controls project origins, metadata fields, retention, public visibility, moderation decisions, exports, and deletion actions. The customer is responsible for its end-user notices, lawful basis, content moderation policy, and configuring only necessary metadata.

## 8. Audit and contact

The dashboard audit log, privacy request records, deployment configuration, and security documentation support compliance review. Replace the operator placeholders and publish a monitored privacy/security contact before accepting customer data.
